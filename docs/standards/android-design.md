# Android design

Android screens follow Android conventions while reaching the same product outcomes. Builds on [mobile design](mobile-design.md).

## Rules

- Follow Android navigation, Back, controls, menus, and presentation conventions while preserving the same product outcomes as other supported platforms.
- Do not copy iOS toolbar or gesture choices when the established Android pattern differs. Use the project's platform adapters.

## Verify

- Back behavior, keyboard dismissal, edge-to-edge insets, larger text, and relevant gestures on Android.

## Exceptions

Record in `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`: rule, scope, replacement, reason.
